Vejlstrup Gårde

Landowner: Jens Burmeister Møller

Address: Holstebrovej 9, 6990 Ulfborg

Status: Active Permission

Last Visited: 14 October 2026

Notes: About 8.5 ha of level farmland south of Ulfborg's 12th-century church. The old Vejlstrup Kro (also called Nyholm Gæstgivergård) stood at the tip of the V by the road fork (Vembvej 2). A gold ring, cooking pits and detector finds are recorded in the V field. First visit: 1875 silver 10 øre and 1867 skilling from the V.

Kronheden

Landowner: Peder Nielsen

Address: Ringkøbingvej 132, 7620 Lemvig

Status: Active Permission

Last Visited: 12 October 2026

Notes: Former heath, first ploughed in the 1900s, on the edge of a burial-mound landscape. An old road to a ford in the Hegedal valley crosses 1bc, with hollow ways on either side. A WWII installation lies on the north edge of 1bc, so treat any ammunition as live. Protected mounds are close by but not on the parcels.

Munkbro Gårde

Landowner: Anne Marie Søndergaard

Address: Munkbrovej 8, 7500 Holstebro

Status: Active Permission

Last Visited: 4 September 2026

Notes: An old crossing of Vegen Å called 'Munkbro' (the monks' bridge), with the old road to Felling church running through the land. About 50 recorded sites lie within 1 km. A ploughed-out mound and flint scatters are recorded on the parcels themselves. Start at the bridge and follow the old road.

Bilsebjerg Gårde

Permission Contact: Louise Nielsen (Daughter)

Address: Skælskørvej 83, 4700 Næstved

Status: Active Permission

Last Visited: 2 September 2026

Notes: A Viking silver hoard and settlement with a 38 m longhouse were excavated about 100 m south of 5a in 2025. Medieval coins and a belt fitting (1200–1499) were found 60 m from the farmhouse in 2024. The south-west corner touches Ting Jellinge village. Report any Viking-age finds from 5a to the museum promptly.
